## Problem Statement

"While the team members are running around trying to meet their targets, their manager has the pressure from higher leadership to ensure that their team performs the best. It is up to the manager to keep track of every member, see who needs a help when they are falling behind and who needs a pat on the back to stay motivated."

"Your task is to show-off your skills to revamp the screens below."

**Core Users:**
1. Sales Agent
2. Sales Manager

> "For the scope of this assignment, i've picked up the sales manager side of it."

---

## UX Audit

"I conducted a thorough UX audit of the current flow to find usability issues and areas of improvement."

**Key Issues (Identified by team):**

- The performance tab screen (dashboard) limits the number of targets shown at a time
- The number of metrics shown on the list page card are high and it's confusing for the user
- Tomorrow, if we want to show more graphs on the details page, how would we structure the page?
- Would adding aspects of gamification enhance the experience?
- The manager has to go through multiple clicks to view data for a user
- How can we help Manager analyse the performance of team members better

---

## Secondary Research

"I looked into several articles and videos online know pain points and understand what information managers look for while analyzing performance of their team."

**Pain Points:**

- Unable to coach or give training to the members falling behind as it's a time consuming process.
- Constant pressure from senior leadership to achieve targets.
- Not getting enough insights on the status of the targets.

**Insights/Findings:**

"Providing insights into both target achievement and individual team member performance allows managers to analyze and evaluate performance more thoroughly."

[Source](https://whaly.io/posts/data-challenges-for-sales-managers-how-to-overcome-them)

## Thing to Ponder Upon

> "What could be the most important thing manager would want to see while analyzing performance of their team?"

**Team level:**
- Overall progress towards achieving targets
- How their team is achieving targets? How many targets are at risk and needs their attention? How many are achieved?

**Individual performance:**
- Overall performance of all the team members
- Who are falling behind and needs utmost attention?
- Who are performing great and deserves appreciation?
- How each member are achieving the targets assigned to them?

## Revamped Designs

"Introducing idea of using tabs to quickly switch between key metrics and their team performance."

### KEY METRICS — Performance Screen

"How might we make it easier for managers to analyse their team performance?"

- Showing overall progress of the targets achieved by their team gives an idea how goals are progressing
- Datapoints for showing amount of targets — at risk, on track, and achieved will help managers make an informed decision and get an general overview
- Along with showing other information like when the deadline is approaching and how many members have achieved the targets

### KEY METRICS — Metric List Screen

_(Detailed metric list view designs)_

### MY TEAM — Performance Screen

**Issue in current experience:**
- The manager has to go through multiple clicks to see the data for a team member
- Unable to track individual team member progress and targets assigned to them

"How might we make access to view data for a team member easier and more accessible?"

**Solution:**
- Making viewing performance of individual members easily accessible with the tab switch
- Showing overall progress of their team giving an idea of where their team stands.
- Showing members who needs manager's attention as nudgeworthy

### MY TEAM — All Member & Individual Member Screens

**Solution:**
- Showing overall progress achieved by the team members and an insight on the status of targets assigned to them.
- Detailed performance view of each team member along with list of all the targets assigned to them.

## Reason for Giving Chat Option in Team Members Card

**Assumption:** "Giving just feature for calling might not be the most suitable, as it could be time-consuming to contact each team member individually. This could disrupt their work. Also direct calling from their manager might also increase stress level in the team members."

"Having an another option of chat, might make it simpler and effective to communicate."

**To validate this, if I had access to data:**
- I would have seen how adoption of calling feature is there by the managers
- Do managers prefer direct calling or async communication. Is it time consuming for them?
- Does direct calling affect the anxiety/stress level of the team members?
- Does direct calling affect the work schedule of the team member?
